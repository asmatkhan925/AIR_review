# Table 5. Evaluation and Robustness Checklist

Purpose: Manuscript-ready draft table for Section 10. Source basis: `05_synthesis_matrices/evaluation_robustness_matrix.csv`, Block G synthesis controls, and `07_draft_sections/10_evaluation_reproducibility_robustness.md`.

| Evaluation risk | Required breakdown or protocol | Why pooled WER/CER is insufficient | Relevant system setting | Reporting requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orthography and transcript normalization | Publish tokenization, punctuation, casing, script, diacritic, spacing, and normalization rules; report raw and normalized scores where feasible | Aggregate scores can change because of formatting conventions rather than recognition quality | Languages with unstable spelling, multiple scripts, morphology, or normalization variation | Provide scoring scripts or exact normalization rules and examples of difficult transcript variants |
| Language, dialect, accent, and variety imbalance | Report language-wise, macro-averaged, dialect-wise, or accent-wise results where metadata permit | Pooled scores can hide failures on tail languages or underrepresented varieties | Multilingual benchmarks, inclusive ASR challenges, accent-aware datasets | State language and variety coverage, averaging policy, and metadata availability |
| Domain, channel, and speech-style mismatch | Break down results by domain, channel, speech style, and deployment condition | Clean read-speech results may not transfer to conversational, telephone, broadcast, classroom, clinical, or field speech | Domain-adapted, continued-pretraining, or deployment-oriented ASR | Report domain composition, channel condition, train/dev/test split construction, and target-domain performance |
| Noise, far-field, overlap, and multi-speaker speech | Include robustness tests for noise, reverberation, overlap, diarization-aware ASR, or far-field recording when relevant | WER/CER on clean audio does not expose degradation under realistic recording conditions | Robust ASR, meeting ASR, far-field, mobile, and low-quality recordings | Report robustness conditions, audio setup, segmentation assumptions, and clean versus degraded results |
| Demographic or speaker-group disparity | Report speaker-group, accent, gender, race, age, or provenance breakdowns only where ethically and locally supported by metadata | Aggregate scores can hide systematic harms to speaker communities | Fairness audits, accent-aware benchmarks, community-facing deployment | Describe metadata source, consent/ethics constraints, subgroup sizes where available, and interpretation limits |
| Adapted foundation-model comparison | Compare against base model and matched adaptation baselines under transparent data and compute conditions | A single score cannot show whether gains come from model scale, adaptation data, decoding, or benchmark fit | Full fine-tuning, continued pretraining, adapters, LoRA/QLoRA, transfer | Report base checkpoint, adaptation data, trainable parameters, hardware, decoding, compute, and language/domain breakdowns |
| Pseudo-labeling or KD pipeline | Evaluate teacher quality, filtering thresholds, retained labels where known, teacher agreement, and student behavior | Aggregate student WER/CER can hide teacher-error amplification or subgroup damage | Self-training, iterative pseudo-labeling, single-teacher KD, multi-teacher KD | Report teacher identity, filtering policy, uncertainty/confidence, disagreement handling, and verified dev/test results |
| Hallucination, over-correction, and contextual leakage | Test unsupported insertions, semantic drift, over-correction, named-entity errors, context leakage, and source grounding | Surface WER/CER may miss fluent but unsupported or semantically altered output | Generative ASR, SpeechLM systems, LLM-assisted correction, contextual ASR | Report correction constraints, context source, N-best/lattice use if any, hallucination checks, and semantic-preservation tests |
| Multimodal and AVSR reliability | Compare audio-only, visual-only where appropriate, and audio-visual results under clean and degraded audio | Audio-only metrics cannot show modality mismatch, missing-modality behavior, or visual-channel dependence | AVSR, multimodal speech recognition, speech-video corpora | Report modality availability, alignment, missing-modality tests, privacy constraints, and language/domain coverage |
| Reproducibility and compute opacity | Provide model versions, recipes, random seeds or variance where relevant, scoring scripts, hardware, memory, training cost, inference cost, and public artifacts where possible | Accuracy without reproducibility and cost cannot support deployability claims in low-resource settings | Foundation-model adaptation, PEFT, benchmarks, pseudo-labeling, and deployment studies | Report reproducibility artifacts, compute/deployment cost, data access constraints, and exact evaluation protocol |
