# Table 2. Dataset and Benchmark Comparison

Purpose: Manuscript-ready draft table for Section 5. Source basis: `05_synthesis_matrices/dataset_benchmark_matrix.csv`, `05_synthesis_matrices/core_60_reference_set.csv`, and `07_draft_sections/05_resources_and_benchmarks_for_low_resource_asr.md`. Watchlist-only resources are excluded from this central comparison table.

| Resource/benchmark | Resource type | Language coverage | Domain/speech style | Supervision/evaluation role | Main limitation or evaluation risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common Voice | Crowdsourced open speech corpus | 29 languages in the paper release; 38 languages collecting | Read speech, crowdsourced | Cross-lingual and low-resource ASR benchmarking; corpus collection and validation example | Quality, accent coverage, and hours vary strongly by language; dialect metadata are limited and inconsistent |
| FLEURS | Multilingual benchmark | 102 languages | Read speech benchmark | ASR, speech language identification, translation, and retrieval evaluation | Limited spontaneous or noisy speech coverage; dialect and deployment conditions remain bounded |
| Babel | Low-resource challenge corpus family | Multiple low-resource languages | Conversational and scripted telephone speech | Low-resource ASR and keyword-search benchmarking | Restricted/licensed access and older recording conditions constrain reuse and comparability |
| MLS | Multilingual speech corpus | Multiple languages | Audiobook read speech | Multilingual ASR training and evaluation | Domain mismatch for conversational, noisy, or deployment speech; local matrix does not support inferred hours |
| VoxPopuli | Multilingual parliamentary speech resource | 23 languages unlabeled; 15 languages transcribed | European Parliament formal speech | Multilingual ASR and SSL evaluation with unlabeled and transcribed components | Language and domain coverage are skewed toward parliamentary speech |
| ML-SUPERB | Multilingual benchmark | 143 languages | Benchmark tasks for ASR and LID | Frozen SSL-feature and shallow downstream evaluation | Benchmark setup constrains conclusions and does not cover all dialect, domain, or fairness axes |
| ML-SUPERB 2.0 | Benchmark extension | 142 languages across 15 datasets | Benchmark tasks | Evaluation across modeling constraints, downstream architectures, adapters, LoRA, and supervised models | Data budgets and task formulations may constrain conclusions beyond the benchmark setting |
| ML-SUPERB 2.0 Challenge | Inclusive hidden-test benchmark | 149 languages and 93 language varieties | Hidden challenge evaluation | Dialect, accent, and language-variety evaluation beyond global averages | Hidden-test comparison helps fairness but does not replace real-world deployment studies |
| Dataset quality audit of Common Voice 17.0, FLEURS, and VoxPopuli | Dataset audit | Multiple public multilingual datasets | Sociolinguistic and quality audit | Evidence for data-quality and reproducibility limitations in multilingual speech datasets | Audits three datasets rather than the full benchmark ecosystem |
| SUPERB | Speech benchmark suite | English speech tasks | General speech benchmark | Foundational speech benchmark context | Not low-resource specific and English-centric |
| XTREME-S | Cross-lingual speech benchmark | Multiple languages | Cross-lingual speech benchmark | Cross-lingual speech representation evaluation | Broader than low-resource ASR alone; low-resource failure analysis still needed |
| IndicSUPERB | Regional speech benchmark suite | Indian languages | Indic speech benchmark | Regional multilingual benchmark context | Details should be verified before strong manuscript use |
| AfriSpeech-200 | Accented speech benchmark resource | 120 accents across 13 countries | Pan-African accented read speech | Accent and fairness evaluation anchor | English-focused, so it supports accent and metadata concerns more than low-resource-language coverage |
| OpenASR20/OpenASR21 | Low-resource challenge benchmarks | 10 low-resource languages in OpenASR20; 15 in OpenASR21 | Conversational telephone speech | Realistic low-resource telephone speech evaluation | Challenge-oriented resources, not broad benchmark families |
