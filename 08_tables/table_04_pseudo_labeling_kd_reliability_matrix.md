# Table 4. Pseudo-Labeling and KD Reliability Matrix

Purpose: Manuscript-ready draft table for Section 9. Source basis: `05_synthesis_matrices/pseudo_labeling_kd_matrix.csv`, Block G synthesis controls, and `07_draft_sections/09_pseudo_labeling_kd_low_resource_asr.md`.

| Supervision condition | Method family | Reliability control | Main failure mode | Evaluation/reporting requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human labels are scarce but a teacher ASR model can decode target speech | Pseudo-label generation | Treat generated transcripts as noisy supervision; document teacher identity, decoding settings, and transcript normalization | Teacher errors, hallucinations, and formatting bias can become training labels | Evaluate pseudo-label quality against a verified dev/test set where possible and report language/domain breakdowns |
| Large pseudo-labeled pools contain uneven-quality labels | Confidence filtering | Filter by confidence, calibration, or staged selection rules | High confidence can still be wrong under domain, dialect, or language mismatch | Report confidence score definition, thresholds, retained-data policy where known, and validation behavior across confidence bins |
| Teacher uncertainty may indicate unreliable labels | Uncertainty filtering | Use uncertainty, dropout disagreement, calibration, or related reliability signals to exclude labels | Uncertainty estimates may be poorly calibrated for the target language or channel | Report uncertainty method, filtering criteria, target-domain validation, and comparison with confidence-only filtering when feasible |
| Abundant unlabeled target speech is available with a minimal evaluation set | Self-training or iterative pseudo-labeling | Iterate teacher/student training while checking whether labels improve or amplify errors | Confirmation bias and repeated relabeling can strengthen early teacher mistakes | Report iteration count, label regeneration policy, filtering, augmentation or LM use if any, and performance by language/domain |
| A compact student or deployment model is needed | Single-teacher KD | Use teacher outputs or representations to train a smaller or adapted student | Student may inherit teacher bias, teacher formatting, and label-space assumptions | Report teacher model, student architecture, KD objective, label-space mapping, accuracy, and deployment cost |
| Multiple teachers or multilingual teachers are available | Multi-teacher KD or agreement-based selection | Use teacher agreement, disagreement, confidence-aware loss weighting, or reproducible selection rules | Teacher disagreement can be hidden, and ensemble outputs may mask systematic errors | Report teacher identities, agreement metrics, disagreement handling, selection policy, and comparison with single-teacher baselines |
| A strong foundation-model teacher is available for an underrepresented language | Foundation-model teacher use or distillation | Use the teacher as a candidate source of supervision, not as proof of label correctness | Large teachers may hallucinate, normalize away valid variants, or perform unevenly across target conditions | Report hallucination/over-correction checks, normalization policy, target-language validation, and limits on central claims when evidence is secondary or watchlist-only |
| Weakly labeled or web-mined speech is available | Pseudo-labeling after source cleaning | First handle segmentation, language identification, alignment, and transcript cleaning | Source noise and label mismatch can be mistaken for useful supervision | Report source filtering, alignment checks, language-ID controls, transcript policy, and separate evaluation on verified references |
