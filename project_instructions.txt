AI Review Paper Project Instructions
====================================

Purpose
-------
Develop a high-quality review paper on Artificial Intelligence for low-resource automatic speech recognition, with Pashto ASR as a focused case study.

Core expectations
-----------------
1. Maintain a traceable workflow from literature search to final manuscript.
2. Use structured paper notes rather than informal summaries.
3. Build synthesis matrices before drafting major comparative claims.
4. Treat Pashto as a case study that clarifies broader low-resource ASR issues.
5. Keep the writing formal, analytical, concise, and evidence-grounded.

Working sequence
----------------
1. Confirm scope and research questions.
2. Run literature searches and record them in search logs.
3. Screen papers using explicit criteria.
4. Add references to references.bib.
5. Write paper notes using the template.
6. Populate synthesis matrices.
7. Draft sections.
8. Build tables and figures.
9. Integrate into the manuscript.
10. Run quality-control checklists before submission.

Do not
------
- Turn the review into a paper-by-paper annotated bibliography.
- Add citations that are not actually used.
- Make unsupported claims about performance, trends, or model superiority.
- Let the Pashto case study dominate the whole review.
- Delete older ideas without archiving them.
