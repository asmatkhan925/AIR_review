Terminology Glossary
====================

ASR
---
Automatic speech recognition; conversion of speech audio into text.

Low-resource ASR
----------------
ASR for languages, domains, dialects, or speaking conditions with limited labeled speech, limited benchmarks, limited tools, or weak data infrastructure.

End-to-end ASR
--------------
Models trained to map acoustic input directly to output tokens using objectives such as CTC, attention-based sequence-to-sequence, or RNN-T.

Self-supervised learning
------------------------
Learning speech representations from unlabeled audio using predictive, contrastive, masked, or reconstruction-style objectives.

Multilingual transfer
---------------------
Using knowledge from one or more source languages to improve ASR in a target language.

Knowledge distillation
----------------------
Training a smaller or target model using outputs from teacher models, often through pseudo-labels or softened predictions.

Foundation speech model
-----------------------
A large pretrained model trained on broad speech data and adapted to multiple tasks or languages.

Speech LLM
----------
A system that connects speech representation or generation with large language model capabilities.

AVSR
----
Audio-visual speech recognition; ASR that uses audio and visual speech cues.

Orthographic normalization
--------------------------
Text preprocessing that reduces spelling, script, punctuation, and symbol variation to improve training and evaluation consistency.

Pashto ASR
----------
Automatic speech recognition for Pashto, used in this review as a focused case study of low-resource ASR challenges.
