# Argument Flow

## Main Argument

ASR has advanced significantly, but low-resource languages remain difficult because the problem is not only data scarcity. Modern AI methods reduce parts of the problem but leave unresolved issues in data quality, dialect coverage, orthography, evaluation, robustness, and deployment cost. Pashto illustrates how these factors interact in a realistic low-resource setting. Future progress requires quality-aware data curation, efficient adaptation, dialect-aware evaluation, multimodal robustness, and community-centered resource development.

## Transition Logic

1. Establish why low-resource ASR matters.
2. Explain how ASR methods evolved.
3. Define the persistent low-resource challenge space.
4. Compare method families by what they solve and what they leave unsolved.
5. Ground the synthesis in Pashto ASR.
6. Extract general research gaps.
7. Propose future directions.
