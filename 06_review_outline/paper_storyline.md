# Paper Storyline

## Narrative

The review begins by showing how ASR moved from classical pipelines to neural, self-supervised, multilingual, foundation-model, and multimodal systems. It then argues that low-resource ASR remains difficult because scarcity interacts with dialect, noise, orthography, domain mismatch, weak evaluation, and limited compute. The method sections explain how major AI paradigms address different parts of this problem. Selective examples from Pashto and other underrepresented languages make the abstract problem concrete without turning the review into a single-language case study. The final sections synthesize research gaps and propose future directions.

## Reader Journey

From model evolution, to persistent challenges, to method families, to comparison, to cross-language examples, to future agenda.
