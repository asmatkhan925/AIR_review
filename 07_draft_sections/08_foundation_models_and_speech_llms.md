# Foundation Models And Speech LLMs

## Purpose Of This Section

Review large pretrained speech models and speech LLM systems, with emphasis on low-resource performance and adaptation.

## Key Arguments To Develop

- Foundation models offer broad multilingual capabilities.
- Performance can be uneven across low-resource languages, dialects, and domains.
- Adaptation methods such as LoRA and adapters may reduce compute barriers.
- Speech LLMs introduce new decoding and interaction possibilities but require careful evaluation.
