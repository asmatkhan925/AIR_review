# Evolution Of ASR

## Purpose Of This Section

Explain how ASR evolved from classical and hybrid systems to end-to-end systems, self-supervised learning, multilingual foundation models, and speech LLMs.

## Key Arguments To Develop

- Classical ASR separated acoustic, pronunciation, and language modeling.
- End-to-end ASR simplified pipelines but increased data requirements.
- SSL shifted value toward unlabeled speech.
- Multilingual and foundation models expanded transfer but did not solve all low-resource issues.
- Speech LLMs introduce new opportunities and evaluation challenges.
