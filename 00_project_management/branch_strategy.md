# Branch Strategy

## Branches

`main`

Stable working version of the review-paper repository.

`draft-sections`

Active section drafting and prose development.

`literature-search`

Literature search logs, screening, references, and paper notes.

`tables-figures`

Tables, synthesis matrices, and figure planning.

`submission-prep`

Final manuscript integration, formatting, cover letter, highlights, and submission materials.

## Commit Guidance

- Keep commits logically grouped.
- Use clear messages, for example: `Add SSL paper notes`, `Draft low-resource challenges section`, or `Update method comparison matrix`.
- Keep `main` readable and stable enough to share.
