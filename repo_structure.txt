Repository Structure
====================

Top-level files
---------------
README.md: project overview and workflow.
AGENTS.md: instructions for Codex and future research agents.
project_instructions.txt: operational instructions for the review project.
review_blueprint.txt: intellectual blueprint of the paper.
writing_guidelines.txt: style and drafting rules.
literature_search_strategy.txt: planned search logic.
citation_policy.txt: rules for citation selection and BibTeX hygiene.
terminology_glossary.txt: abbreviations and working definitions.
repo_structure.txt: this structure guide.

Major folders
-------------
00_project_management: timeline, tasks, revisions, decisions, and reviewer response preparation.
01_scope_and_planning: scope, questions, criteria, contribution, reader profile, and journal requirements.
02_literature_search: keywords, queries, database sources, and search/screening logs.
03_references: BibTeX database and curated reference categories.
04_paper_notes: structured notes by theme.
05_synthesis_matrices: comparison and gap-analysis matrices.
06_review_outline: outline, dependency map, argument flow, and storyline.
07_draft_sections: section-level drafting workspace.
08_tables: planned tables for the review.
09_figures: planned figures and figure workspaces.
10_case_study_pashto: Pashto-specific case-study materials.
11_quality_control: checklists for review quality and submission readiness.
12_manuscript: integrated manuscript and submission materials.
13_appendices: supplementary materials.
14_archive: old drafts, rejected structures, and outdated notes.
