Writing Guidelines
==================

Style
-----
Use formal, analytical, critical, concise, and coherent prose suitable for a high-impact review journal.

Prefer
------
- Thematic grouping.
- Comparative analysis.
- Clear topic sentences.
- Strong transitions.
- Explicit limitations.
- Research gaps grounded in evidence.
- Tables and figures that support synthesis.

Avoid
-----
- Paper-by-paper listing.
- Unsupported claims.
- Overgeneralization.
- Repetition across sections.
- Informal language.
- Excessive bullet points in final prose.

Section drafting rule
---------------------
Each draft section should begin with scaffolding:
1. Purpose of this section.
2. Key arguments to develop.
3. Literature to include.
4. Expected tables or figures.
5. Drafting notes.
6. Open questions.

Final prose rule
----------------
Once evidence is collected, convert bullet scaffolds into connected academic paragraphs. A good section should explain relationships between methods, contradictions in the literature, limitations of existing work, and the relevance of the section to the full review argument.
