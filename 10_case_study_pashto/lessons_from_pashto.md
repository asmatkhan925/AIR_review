# Lessons From Pashto

## General Lessons

- Low-resource ASR is not only about data size.
- Orthography affects both training and evaluation.
- Teacher diversity can matter when pseudo-labels are noisy.
- Multimodal cues need quality control.
- Data pipeline transparency is essential.
