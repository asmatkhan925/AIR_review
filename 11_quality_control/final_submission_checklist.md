# Final Submission Checklist

Before submission, confirm:

- Title is final.
- Abstract is final.
- Keywords are prepared.
- Highlights are prepared if required.
- Main manuscript is complete.
- References are complete and formatted.
- Figures are final and high resolution.
- Tables are numbered and cited.
- Cover letter is drafted.
- Author information is correct.
- Journal formatting requirements are verified.
- Supplementary materials are ready.
