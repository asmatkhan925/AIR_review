# Claim Strength Checklist

Use this checklist before moving any major claim into manuscript prose.

## Rule

No major claim enters the manuscript unless it appears in `05_synthesis_matrices/evidence_to_claim_matrix.csv`.

## Claim Strength Categories

| Strength | Meaning | Required Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | Supported by multiple empirical studies, benchmarks, or high-quality reviews. | Multiple verified papers, clear agreement, known limitations stated. |
| Moderate | Supported by some evidence, but coverage is incomplete or context-dependent. | At least one verified empirical source plus supporting discussion. |
| Interpretation | A reasoned synthesis or forward-looking argument. | Clearly marked as interpretation and not phrased as settled fact. |

## Wording Guide

| Strength | Suitable Wording |
|---|---|
| Strong | The evidence indicates; multiple studies show; benchmark results consistently suggest. |
| Moderate | Available studies suggest; current evidence indicates; results remain context-dependent. |
| Interpretation | This review argues; a plausible interpretation is; this points to a need for further validation. |
