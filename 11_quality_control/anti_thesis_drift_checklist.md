# Anti-Thesis-Drift Checklist

Use this checklist before accepting any section as part of the review manuscript.

## Core Rule

The review is not about proving that my thesis is important. The review is about explaining why low-resource ASR remains unsolved in the foundation-model era, using my thesis expertise to organize the field critically.

## Primary Test

For every section, ask:

Is this section about low-resource ASR generally, or is it only explaining my Pashto work?

If the answer is only Pashto, move it to a case box, example paragraph, or appendix-style discussion.

## Pashto Use Rules

Pashto material is appropriate when it:

- Illustrates a broader low-resource ASR problem.
- Shows how dialect variation affects evaluation.
- Shows how Arabic-derived script normalization affects transcripts.
- Demonstrates pseudo-label reliability problems.
- Motivates multimodal or noisy-speech robustness.

Pashto material is not appropriate when it:

- Replaces broader evidence from other low-resource languages.
- Turns a review section into a thesis chapter.
- Describes implementation details that are not needed for field-level synthesis.
- Makes claims that are not compared with wider low-resource ASR literature.
