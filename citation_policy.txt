Citation Policy
===============

Purpose
-------
Citations should support the review argument, not inflate the bibliography.

Rules
-----
1. Cite seminal papers for foundational concepts.
2. Cite recent papers for current trends.
3. Prefer top-tier venues and high-quality journals when multiple sources support the same claim.
4. Include influential preprints only when they are highly relevant, and identify their status carefully.
5. Every included paper should serve a clear role in the review.
6. Every factual or evaluative claim in the manuscript should be supported.
7. Keep `03_references/references.bib` clean, deduplicated, and consistently formatted.

Reference workflow
------------------
1. Add candidate papers to the relevant reference list in `03_references/`.
2. Add BibTeX entries to `references.bib`.
3. Create a paper note if the paper will be discussed in detail.
4. Add evidence from the paper to the relevant synthesis matrix.
5. Cite the paper in draft prose only after its role is clear.

Missing references
------------------
Use `03_references/missing_references.md` for papers mentioned in notes or drafts but not yet added to BibTeX.
