Literature Search Strategy
==========================

Objective
---------
Identify seminal, recent, and case-study-relevant literature for a review on AI methods for low-resource ASR.

Search categories
-----------------
1. Classical and end-to-end ASR.
2. Self-supervised speech representation learning.
3. Multilingual and cross-lingual ASR.
4. Low-resource ASR adaptation.
5. Knowledge distillation and pseudo-labeling.
6. Foundation speech models.
7. Speech large language models.
8. Multimodal and audio-visual ASR.
9. Datasets and benchmarks.
10. Pashto and related low-resource language studies.
11. Evaluation, normalization, and fairness.

Databases
---------
Use Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ACL Anthology, ISCA Archive, SpringerLink, ScienceDirect, arXiv, and Papers With Code.

Screening procedure
-------------------
1. Record the search in `02_literature_search/search_log.csv`.
2. Export candidate titles and metadata where possible.
3. Apply inclusion and exclusion criteria.
4. Record decisions in `02_literature_search/screening_log.csv`.
5. Track rejected but potentially useful papers in `02_literature_search/rejected_papers_log.csv`.

Search quality notes
--------------------
Use broad searches first, then targeted searches by method family, model name, dataset, language, and evaluation issue. Repeat searches near submission to include recent 2025-2026 work.
