# Survey Papers

## Survey Types To Collect

- ASR surveys.
- Low-resource NLP surveys.
- Self-supervised speech surveys.
- Multimodal learning surveys.
- Speech foundation-model surveys.

| Survey | Year | Scope | Strength | Limitation | Use in review |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Besacier, Barnard, Karpov, and Schultz, "Automatic speech recognition for under-resourced languages: A survey" | 2014 | Low-resource ASR before SSL and foundation models | Historical baseline for under-resourced ASR problem framing | Predates SSL, foundation models, and modern multilingual benchmarks | Introduction; What Makes ASR Low-Resource |
| Prabhavalkar et al., "End-to-End Speech Recognition: A Survey" | 2024 | End-to-end ASR architectures | Strong modern taxonomy of CTC, attention, transducer, decoding, and LM integration | Broad ASR survey rather than low-resource-specific | From Hybrid ASR to Foundation Speech Models |
| Cui et al., "Recent Advances in Speech Language Models: A Survey" | 2025 | SpeechLM and speech-language foundation models | Strong bridge to Speech-LLM and LLM-assisted ASR directions | Not low-resource-specific and fast-moving | Multimodal, Speech-LLM, and LLM-Assisted Low-Resource ASR |
