# Expected Reader Profile

## Primary Readers

- AI researchers.
- Speech-processing researchers.
- NLP researchers.
- Low-resource language technology researchers.
- Multimodal learning researchers.

## Reader Needs

- A clear map of major ASR method families.
- A critical explanation of why low-resource ASR remains difficult.
- Comparative tables that clarify trade-offs.
- Practical insight from a concrete case study.
- Future directions that are specific enough to guide research.

## Writing Implication

The paper should explain specialist concepts clearly without oversimplifying technical trade-offs.
