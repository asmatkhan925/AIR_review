# Inclusion And Exclusion Criteria

## Inclusion Criteria

- Direct relevance to ASR, low-resource speech recognition, multilingual ASR, speech representation learning, foundation speech models, knowledge distillation, multimodal ASR, or Pashto ASR.
- Seminal papers regardless of date if they define major methods or evaluation concepts.
- Recent papers from 2020-2026 for current trends, model families, benchmarks, and low-resource evaluations.
- Dataset and benchmark papers that influence low-resource ASR evaluation.
- High-quality preprints if influential, technically relevant, or not yet superseded by peer-reviewed versions.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Papers unrelated to ASR or speech-language technology.
- Papers focused only on non-ASR speech tasks unless they contribute methods or datasets relevant to ASR.
- Low-quality or unclear sources with insufficient methodological detail.
- Duplicates, superseded workshop versions, or papers with no clear role in the review.

## Screening Notes

Record decisions in `02_literature_search/screening_log.csv`. For excluded papers that may still be useful later, use `02_literature_search/rejected_papers_log.csv`.
