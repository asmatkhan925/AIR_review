# Review Scope

## Main Topic

Artificial Intelligence methods for low-resource automatic speech recognition, with emphasis on methods, challenges, trends, and future directions.

## Secondary Topics

- End-to-end ASR.
- Self-supervised speech learning.
- Multilingual and cross-lingual transfer.
- Knowledge distillation and pseudo-labeling.
- Foundation speech models and speech LLMs.
- Multimodal and audio-visual ASR.
- Evaluation, normalization, fairness, and robustness.

## Focused Case Study

Pashto ASR will be used to illustrate how low-resource challenges interact in practice: limited labeled data, dialect diversity, orthographic normalization, noisy speech, and dataset constraints.

## Excluded Or Limited Topics

- Speech enhancement as a standalone field, unless directly tied to ASR robustness.
- General NLP for low-resource languages, unless it informs ASR.
- Speaker recognition, emotion recognition, and speech synthesis, unless relevant to ASR systems or datasets.

## Target Contribution

The review should offer a taxonomy, challenge synthesis, method comparison, Pashto case study, research gaps, and future research agenda.
