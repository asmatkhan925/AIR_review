# Figure Plan

This is the active four-figure plan for the AIR review. Figure source drafts are editable SVG files in `09_figures/`.

## Figure 1: Foundation-Model-Era Low-Resource ASR Bottleneck Stack

Source draft: `09_figures/figure_01_bottleneck_stack.svg`

Supports Sections 1, 3, 12, and 13. Shows that foundation speech models improve the starting point but leave interacting bottlenecks in resources, language fit, adaptation, supervision, evaluation, robustness, compute, reporting, and multimodal or LLM-assisted risks.

## Figure 2: Six-Layer Taxonomy of Low-Resource ASR

Source draft: `09_figures/figure_02_six_layer_taxonomy.svg`

Supports Section 6 and the synthesis in Section 12. Shows the six interacting taxonomy layers: Resource, Language, Model, Adaptation, Supervision, and Evaluation.

## Figure 3: Cross-Block Evidence Flow

Source draft: `09_figures/figure_03_cross_block_evidence_flow.svg`

Supports Sections 4, 8, 10, and 12. Shows evidence flow from resource and language conditions through model choice, adaptation, and supervision into evaluation, robustness, and the future agenda, with feedback loops.

## Figure 4: Future Agenda Map for Reliable Low-Resource ASR

Source draft: `09_figures/figure_04_future_agenda_map.svg`

Supports Section 13. Shows the future research agenda around reliable low-resource ASR, including definitions, documentation, orthography and dialect benchmarks, compute-aware adaptation, reliable pseudo-labeling and KD, evaluation, AVSR/multimodal robustness, constrained LLM assistance, reproducibility, and deployment.
