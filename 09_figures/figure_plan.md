## Assembly Rules

- Figures 1-4 are the active manuscript-facing figure set.
- Four editable SVG source files exist and remain the source assets.
- PNG and PDF previews exist for review and layout checking.
- Figure insertion should happen only after the corresponding manuscript sections are assembled and the table/figure callouts can be checked in context.
- Figures should be referenced as argumentative aids that support the review synthesis, not as decorative images.
- Do not add citations, long captions, or detailed evidence notes inside the SVG files; keep captions and alt text in `09_figures/figure_captions_and_alt_text.md`.

## Legacy Placeholders

Older placeholder directories named `fig_01_*` through `fig_06_*`, if still present, are legacy placeholders or source ideas from an earlier six-figure plan. They should not guide final manuscript assembly unless deliberately revived in a later decision-log entry.
