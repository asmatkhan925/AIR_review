# Figure Asset Policy

## Principles

- Use original figures created for this review whenever possible.
- Avoid reusing published figures unless permission is obtained.
- Keep figure captions in the manuscript, not embedded inside images.
- Store editable source files for figures.
- Name final figures as `Fig1`, `Fig2`, `Fig3`, and so on for submission.

## Formats

- Use editable vector sources where possible.
- Export vector figures as PDF, EPS, or SVG for editing.
- Follow Springer guidance for final submission formats when preparing upload files.
- Use TIFF or other required raster formats only when the figure type requires it.

## Quality

- Ensure high contrast.
- Ensure lettering is readable at final size.
- Use consistent fonts and sizes.
- Do not include figure titles inside the image.
- Use patterns or direct labels where color alone would reduce accessibility.

## Permissions

Record permission status for any adapted or reused material before submission.
